## 1. Rules the video must follow

From the 2026 Space Apps Project Submission Guide:

- **30 seconds or less, including the end card.** Longer videos are not eligible for Global Judging. Aim for
  29 seconds.
- **English:** English voice-over and English subtitles (we use both).
- **No one under 18:** no faces, voices, hands or names of anyone under 18. Record the **screen only**. The voice-over
  must be read by someone 18 or older, or use a text-to-speech voice (then say so in the watermark, see below).
- **SI watermark:** our sprites were modified with SI and most of the sounds are AI-generated, so a visible watermark
  must stay on screen for the **whole video**: `SI-assisted art & audio`. If the voice-over is a text-to-speech or
  AI voice, use `SI-assisted art, audio & voice-over`.
- **No NASA logos**, insignia or mission patches added in the edit. Writing data names as text ("NASA DONKI") is fine.
- **Public link** that needs no login (YouTube unlisted or public, or Google Drive "Anyone with the link").
- Show the **team name and members**, the **challenge** and the **project name**.

## 2. Before recording

**Computer and browser**

- Use a laptop or desktop, Google Chrome, screen resolution **1920 × 1080**, browser zoom **100%**.
- Open https://arjundip173.github.io/RedSoil-Outpost-7/ and press **F11** for full screen.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb, close other apps and tabs, and stay connected to the internet (Ask LUNA needs it).

**Recorder** (OBS Studio, Xbox Game Bar on Windows, or QuickTime / Cmd+Shift+5 on Mac)

- 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, record **game sound** (system audio).
- **Microphone off.** The voice-over is recorded separately (section 5), so the raw take has no talking or noise.

**Game settings** (main menu → Settings)

- Sound and music **on**, volume around 80%, LUNA's voice **on**, text size **Big**.
- Start a **New Mission** (not Continue) so the rocket launch plays. Name the astronaut something fun and fictional,
  such as **Nova**. Never use a real child's name.

**Markers**

- Keep the recorder's clock visible to one teammate, who fills in the [marker sheet](#4-marker-sheet-fill-in-while-recording)
  whenever a shot starts and ends. This is what the editor uses to find the best moments in the long take.
- Before each important shot, **stand still for 2 seconds**. These pauses make clean cut points.

## 6. Editing style

Fun, quick and playful, like the game itself:

- **Look:** pixel-art style titles in the game's font **Press Start 2P** (free on Google Fonts); captions in **VT323**
  or another clear font. Colours from the game: Mars orange `#c1440e`, gold `#ffd166`, cyan `#7fe3ff`, dark
  `#120a0a`, white text with a dark outline.
- **Pace:** a new clip every 2 to 3 seconds, cuts on the beat of the music. Speed up walking and driving (2× to 4×),
  never the moments where text has to be read.
- **Motion:** punch-in zooms (110% to 130%) on the key moment of each clip; titles "pop" in (scale 80% → 105% →
  100%) with a game blip sound; quick pixel or mosaic wipes, or plain hard cuts. One small glitch effect for the
  solar flare only.
- **Sound:** the game's calm music under the whole video, switching to the tense track for the solar flare and back.
  Lower the music under the voice-over (about -12 dB). Use the game's own sound effects on cuts (blip, spark, scan,
  alarm, victory).
- **Subtitles:** burned-in English subtitles of the voice-over, bottom centre, never covering the key action.
- **Watermark:** `SI-assisted art & audio` in the top-right corner for the whole video, small (about 3% of the frame
  height), white at 70% opacity.
- Keep the game's own HUD readable; avoid heavy filters, colour shifts or emoji stickers that hide the pixel art.
