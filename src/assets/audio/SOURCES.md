# Sound sources

Most of the sound effects and the music in Red Soil: Outpost 7 were generated with AI audio tools for this game by
team SideLoaders. Each file also carries this note in its own metadata (the WAV INFO comment or the MP3 ID3 comment),
written by `scripts/mark-si-assets.py`. LUNA's voice is not a recording: the browser speaks her lines with its
built-in text-to-speech.
